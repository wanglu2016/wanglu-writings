上个月，我开始翻阅《俱舍》的两个英译本，并随手记下错误。因为一开始只打算翻翻颂，看看阿毗达磨术语用英文怎么表达，所以长行基本上是跳过去的，碰到复杂一点的地方，才会看看英文长行对不对。
我记下错误的方式非常简单粗暴，就是直接把有问题的句子复制下来，如果句子长，甚至只复制半个句子，加个按语说“某某词错了”，就完了。非常简单省事，写汉语跟写英语是一样的，只需要写“is wrong”就行。后来，丁一老师帮助我核对法文和梵文，并补充了英文解释。这之后，我就把活儿干得细一点了。所以，第六品和第八品，我基本上把长行都读了，少数尾注也读了。
第六品和第八品里，找出了38个问题，又从第七品里拽出两条，凑够40个。40个已经不算太少了。而且我最近的劲头也不是很大了，所以先把它们贴出来。第一二品的10个问题，我们贴在了H-Buddhism上。加上这里的40条，就50条了。
前两天，我和许国明（水果）在毗昙群里讨论欲界色的法后得问题，他纠正了我的一个误解。我之前以为欲界色的法后得是限于无表的，实际上表色也有（这个问题在应用上很值得展开聊聊，回头有空再写）。每当琢磨这些问题的时候，我会觉得比读英文《俱舍》或者现当代的阿毗达磨论文更费劲——是费劲得多。我以前学阿毗达磨，主要是通过这种办法的。经常会有一个问题卡住，三天想不通，甚至三个月想不通，乃至半年一年都没有准确理解和消化的。
也就是在这种困顿中，进步是最大的。你哪怕不能得到想要的问题结论，但在探索过程中，会对一些知识理解得更清晰，纠正先前的误解也是家常便饭。而读论文，可以读得很快，英文论文，写notes的话，可以一天一篇；中文的话，两小时就差不多了。但对学习阿毗达磨的帮助，是非常有限的。不过读论文会比琢磨问题轻松，而且容易满足虚荣心，比较好读下去。琢磨问题的话，一开始是有兴趣的，而一旦卡住没有进展，慢慢就有点灰心了，还会挫败。虽然在挫败中总归知道这是进步的必由之路，但头一懵还是想睡觉或者搞别的了。
琢磨问题的话，除了问题本身带来的激励，是很少有别的激励的。不过，我认为，这是学习阿毗达磨的正确道路。前提是，问题确实是问题（这是另外的话题）。但抛开“我认为”，这种方式在今天的阿毗达磨研究当中，是绝对的非主流。有时候，我会想到别的专业工作的验证标准，比如计算机或者工程，他们是可以在应用上见分晓的。就像两辆车，谁跑得快，遛遛就知道了。所谓“少废话，show me the code”。但有些领域，很多时候是没有code的。
1.
Pruden (6-19a-b 長行pp. 932-933)
Strong Patience exists when the ascetic considers, in a single thought, only one aspect [=impermanence] applied to the sufferings of Kamadhatu. Such is the explanation of the Vaibhasi- kas.
Lodrö Sangpo (6-19ab 長行，p. 1914)
When the practitioners consider, in one single thought, no more than one aspect or mode of activity ("impermanent") applied to the unsatisfactoriness of the realm of desire, that is the strong receptivity.
《阿毘達磨俱舍論》卷23：「從此位無間起勝善根一行一剎那名上品忍」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1558, p. 119c11-12)
兩處不必然是"impermanent"行相 。其實，前面的"they apply their mind to no more than two aspects or modes of activity ("impermanent", "unsatisfactory") "也錯了，因為是同類型的問題，不單獨列出來了。
《阿毘達磨俱舍論》卷23：「世第一法，緣欲苦諦法念住現在修未來四，隨一行相現在修未來四，無異分故、似見道故。」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1558, p. 120a7-9)
《阿毘達磨大毘婆沙論》卷109：「又見行者復有二種。著我見者依非我行相入正性離生。著我所見者依空行相入正性離生。諸愛行者亦有二種。我慢增者依非常行相入正性離生。懈怠增者依苦行相入正性離生。」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T27, no. 1545, pp. 563c26-564a2)
2.
Pruden (6-22d 長行pp. 938-939)
Loss through falling necessarily results from the defilements; but not unqualified loss {vihdni), which can result from a quality, for example, the loss of the quality of Prthagjana at the production of the Path, etc.
Lodrö Sangpo (6-22d 長行p. 1920)
Loss-through-retrogressing necessarily results from defilements [do,m; klesa]; but not unqualified (na avasyam) loss (vihani), which can result from something good (gu,:ta), for example, the loss of the status of ordinary worldling when producing the (noble) path, etc.
《阿毘達磨俱舍論》卷23：「失退二捨非得為性，退必起過，失不必然。」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1558, p. 120b20-21)
兩個英譯本的例子錯了，「見道」根本不會捨此善根。
《阿毘達磨俱舍論》卷23：「聖由失地捨， 異生由命終， 初二亦退捨， 依本必見諦」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1558, p. 120a14-15)
《阿毘達磨俱舍論》卷23：「然除彼得，勿諸聖者煖等善根重現前故」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1558, p. 119c18-19)
3.
Pruden (6-23b 長行p. 940)
and those defilements which are abandoned by the Seeing of the Truths.
Lodrö Sangpo (6-22d 長行p. 1921)
defilements (klesa), those that have been abandoned by insight into the truths. (MYS, 165b6) <175>
《阿毘達磨俱舍論》卷23：「惑謂見所斷惑」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1558, p. 120c8-9)
由於「忍位」在見道之前，這裡想說的是「那些『見所斷的煩惱』從現在開始就永遠不會再生起了」，不是說「已經被見道斷除的煩惱」。有必要補充的兩點是：1,「見所斷惑」不一定都是通過「見道」斷除的。2，「見所斷惑」中，有一部分是有可能在「忍位」之前由世俗道斷除的，即有頂之外的「見所斷惑」。不管「見所斷惑」此時有沒有斷除，斷除了多少，它們從「上忍位」開始，就再也不會生起。
4.
Lodrö Sangpo (6-22d 長行p. 1921)
through weak receptivity, unfortunate planes of existence enter into the condition destined to arise;
《阿毘達磨俱舍論》卷23：「此於下上位，隨所應而得，謂於下忍得惡趣不生，所餘不生至上忍方得。」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1558, p. 120c9-10)
應該是not to arise, Pruden (6-23b 長行p. 940)是對的：through weak Patience, the painful realms of rebirth enter into the condition of not arising。
5.
Pruden (6-24a-b note148, p. 1054)
148. Hsiian-tsang: After having produced the first two nirvedhabhdgiyas, he can change to another vehicle.
Lodrö Sangpo (6-24a-b note271, p. 2078)
LVP: Hstian-tsang: After having produced the first two stages conducive to penetration, they can change to another vehicle.
《阿毘達磨俱舍論》卷23：「有餘獨覺異麟角喻，起彼種性初二善根，轉向餘乘，理無遮礙。」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1558, p. 121a3-5)
《阿毘達磨俱舍釋論》卷17：「若有人執，有別獨覺異犀角喻獨覺，於彼人轉二善根成菩薩。此義無遮。」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1559, p. 273a17-19)
玄奘的意思是，除了麟角喻之外的獨覺，可以在初二善根（即煖善根或頂善根）的階段轉向餘乘，也就是說，可以在生起第三善根（忍善根）之前轉向餘乘；而不是說在生起初二善根之後轉向餘乘。
6.
Pruden (7-1c, p. 1088)
This refers to the eight abhtsamaya knowledges (duhkhe jridna, etc., vi. 26) [and also to all prajnd of the pure Path of Meditation up to the Knowledge of Extinction].
Lodrö Sangpo (7-1c, p. 2171)
This refers to the eight cognitions of direct realization (abhisamaya) (cognition with regard to unsatisfactoriness [dub,khe jiiiina], etc.; vi. 26) (and also to any understanding of the pure path of cultivation up to [but not including] the cognition of exhaustion).
《阿毘達磨俱舍論》卷26〈7 分別智品〉：「所餘皆通智見二性」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1558, p. 134b27-28)
《阿毘達磨俱舍釋論》卷19〈7 分別慧品〉：「釋曰：異於忍及盡智無生智，所餘無流般若，亦見亦智。」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1559, p. 285c18-19)
兩個英譯本中對「所餘」進行了解釋。從解釋看，他們都把「現觀」理解為等同於「見道」了，實際上，其中的「道類智」屬於修道。更重要的是，他們都沒有提到「無學道」的諸智。
7.
Pruden (6-32, 長行, p. 955)
As a consequence, the abider in a result, as long as he does not exert himself with a view to a higher progress for the acquisition of a new state, that is, as long as he does not cultivate the preparatory exercises which have for their result the abandoning of the defilements not yet abandoned and which is the cause of the acquisition of a new state, this resident is not a candidate for this new state.
Lodrö Sangpo (6-32, 長行, p. 1938)
Consequently, the abiders in the fruit, as long as they do not devote themselves (prayujyate) to a higher progress (vise~iiya) in mind, for the acquisition of a new fruit, i.e., as long as they do not cultivate the preparatory effort (prayoga) which has for its result the abandonment of defilements not yet abandoned and which is the cause for the acquisition of a new fruit, so long are these abiders not approachers of this new fruit.
《阿毘達磨俱舍論》卷23：「然諸先斷欲界修惑一至五等，至得果時，此生必定起勝果道。」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1558, p. 122c23-24)
“the defilements not yet abandoned”错了。这里之所以「此生必定起勝果道」，不是此生要進斷尚未斷的煩惱，而是對見道前以世俗道斷的煩惱(非果道攝者)起無漏離繫得。
8.
Pruden (6-32, 長行, p. 956)
Nevertheless, the ascetic who, [previously] detached from the Third Dhyana [by a worldly path], enters into niydma (vi.26a) or the Path of Seeing in a sphere lower than this Dhyana [that is, in anagamya, the First or Second Dhyana, or an intermediate state], certainly realizes a path higher than the result.
Lodrö Sangpo (6-32, 長行, p. 1938)
Nevertheless, the practitioners who, (previously) detached from the third meditation (dhyiina) (through a mundane path), enter into the assurance (niyiima; vi. 26a) or the path of insight (darsanamiirga) by relying on a stage lower than this meditation (i.e., by relying on the preparatory meditation [aniigamya], the first and second meditation, the intermediate meditation [dhyiiniintara]), certainly realize a path higher than the fruit (phalavisi~fa'!l miirgam).
《阿毘達磨俱舍論》卷23：「由此先離三靜慮染後依下地入見道者，彼得果已於現生中必能引生後勝果道。」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1558, p. 122c24-27)
"Nevertheless"錯了，這裡不是轉折關係，是解釋說明前面的原則，即玄奘的“由此”。
9.
Pruden (6-32, note199, p. 1060)
It is certain that this ascetic acquires, by means of the Path of Seeing, a pure sukhendriya of the sphere of the Third Dhyana;
Lodrö Sangpo (6-32, note375, p. 2105)
It is certain that these practitioners acquire, by means of the path of insight, a pure faculty of sensation of pleasure (sukhendriya) of the sphere of the third meditation;
《阿毘達磨俱舍論》卷23：「由此先離三靜慮染後依下地入見道者，彼得果已於現生中必能引生後勝果道。若異此者，聖生上地應不可說定成樂根。」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1558, p. 122c24-27)
《阿毘達磨俱舍釋論》卷17：「若人離欲第三定，更依下地[1]入正定，此人必定現前由果勝道。若不爾，從下生上界則不應與樂根相應」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1559, p. 275a3-5)[1] 入【大】，人【宋】
實際上，這種情況（「先離三靜慮染後依下地入見道」），在見道階段，恰恰是不能得「第三靜慮無漏樂根」的——這正是為什麼「彼得果已於現生中必能引生後勝果道」的原因，假如通過見道得了無漏樂根，就不需要再「起勝果道」得無漏樂根了。兩個注釋隨後的解釋邏輯也是錯誤的。
10.
Lodrö Sangpo (6-38ab, p. 1951)
Those who are called half-leapers (ardhapluta) are the noble ones who, leaping over only one place,452 { 4 b} enter among those of the Highest (Akani~thas) after being reborn among those of the pure abodes.
《阿毘達磨俱舍論》卷24：「言半超者，從彼漸次生下淨居，乃至中間能越一處生色究竟，超非全故名為半超。」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1558, p. 124c14-16)
《阿毘達磨俱舍釋論》卷17：「半超出者，從初定生淨居已，乃至超一別處，方生阿迦尼師吒。」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1559, p. 276b20-21)
"only one" 錯了，實際上，超任意一處即可，超多處亦可。Pruden (p. 968) "passing beyond any one place"，是合適的。
《阿毘達磨大毘婆沙論》卷174：「半超者謂欲界歿生梵眾天。梵眾天歿於上一切天處或更生一處或二或三或四或五。乃至或唯超一處遍生餘處然後生色究竟。或生非想非非想處而般涅槃。」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T27, no. 1545, p. 875c8-12)

Pruden (6-41a-b, 長行, p. 973)
But the Aryan who obtains the state of Anagamin after having been reborn in Rupadhatu, goes sometimes to Arupyadhatu as a Bhavagraparana Urdhvasrota.

《阿毘達磨俱舍論》卷24：「若於色界經生聖者，容有上生無色界義，如行色界極有頂者。」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1558, p. 125b22-23)
《阿毘達磨俱舍釋論》卷17：「於色界中，若轉生聖人，有時入無色界。若上流，有頂為勝。」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1559, p. 277a14-15)

Lodrö Sangpo (6-41cd, 長行, p. 1957)

Lodrö Sangpo (6-44d, 長行, p. 1965)
For the formless meditative attainments, there are twenty, thirty-two and forty-eight [adamantine concentrations].
《阿毘達磨俱舍論》卷24：「空處四十，識處三十二，無所有處二十四。」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1558, p. 126c14-15)

15.
Pruden (6-45c-d, 長行, p. 985)
the worldly path of a certain sphere cannot be opposed to this same sphere
Lodrö Sangpo (6-45c-d, 長行, p. 1969)
the mundane path of a certain stage [bhumi] cannot be opposed to this same stage,
《阿毘達磨俱舍論》卷24：「自地不能治自地故」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1558, p. 127a25-26)
《阿毘達磨俱舍釋論》卷17：「依自地道非對治故」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1559, p. 278b28-29)

16.
Pruden (6-45c-d, 長行, p. 986)
This possession is worldly and transworldly.
Lodrö Sangpo (6-46ab, 長行, p. 1969)
This possession is mundane [laukika] and supramundane [lokottara].
《阿毘達磨俱舍論》卷24：「論曰：諸有學聖用有漏道離下八地修斷染時，能具引生二離繫得。」(CBETA 2023.Q1, T29, no. 1558, p. 127b4-5)
